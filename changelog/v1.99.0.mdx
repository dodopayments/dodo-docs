## Improvements

### 1. **Checkout Payment Page Overhaul**

The payment page on checkout has been substantially reworked end-to-end — tighter layout, faster perceived load, clearer validation states, and refined card-input UX. In aggregate, these changes lifted **observed checkout success rates by ~2–3%** across global traffic.

What changed:

- **Smoother field interactions** — autofocus, smarter tab order, and improved keyboard navigation on the card form
- **Cleaner error and loading states** — inline validation surfaces precisely where the customer needs to act, replacing the previous full-form error banner pattern
- **Faster paint** — skeletons and progressive hydration eliminate the brief blank-state flicker on slow networks
- **Mobile polish** — tap targets, scroll behavior, and keyboard handling tuned specifically for mobile checkout, where the majority of traffic now lives

<Tip>
No integration changes are required. Existing checkout sessions automatically pick up the new payment page.
</Tip>

### 2. **Product Form Rework**

The product **create**, **edit**, and **duplicate** flows have been rebuilt from the ground up around a single, consistent form experience.

Highlights:

- **Live preview** — see how your product appears on the checkout and customer portal as you edit, side-by-side with the form
- **Autosave** — drafts are persisted automatically, so navigating away or losing your tab no longer means losing your work
- **Markdown editor** — product descriptions now support a full markdown editor with live rendering, link previews, and inline formatting controls
- **Duplicate flow parity** — duplicating a product opens the same unified form pre-filled, instead of a stripped-down dialog, so you can adjust every field before saving the copy

<Tip>
Use **Duplicate** to spin up regional or pricing-tier variants of an existing product without re-entering descriptions, metadata, or fulfilment configuration.
</Tip>

### 3. **Business Settings Page Redesign**

The **Settings → Business** page has been redesigned to make configuration easier to scan and faster to update. Settings are now grouped into clearer sections (business profile, payment behaviour, adaptive currency, dispute handling, communications), each with explanatory copy that explains the impact of every toggle before you flip it.

There are **no behavioural changes** to existing settings — only the layout, grouping, and surrounding explanations have improved.

### 4. **New Customer Communication Email Toggles**

Granular toggles for the customer-facing email notifications introduced in the [previous release](/features/emails) are now available under **Settings → Business → Communications**. Turn individual emails on or off without touching merchant-wide email defaults.

You can independently control transactional emails for payments, subscription lifecycle events, recovery flows, license keys, and entitlement grants, so the customer experience matches your brand's communication strategy.

<Tip>
If you handle your own customer notifications via webhooks, disable the matching Dodo Payments email to avoid sending customers duplicate messages for the same event.
</Tip>

### 5. **Entitlements & Credits UI Improvements**

The Entitlements and Credits dashboards have been refined for clarity at scale:

- **Metadata support** — view and edit metadata on entitlements and credit grants directly from the dashboard, matching what's available via the API
- **Cleaner grant detail views** — lifecycle timeline, revocation reason, and platform integration status are now grouped into a single, easier-to-read panel
- **Better filter and sort behaviour** — list views now persist filters across navigation, consistent with the rest of the dashboard

<Tip>
Use entitlement metadata to tag grants by campaign, cohort, or internal reference for downstream reporting and reconciliation.
</Tip>

Learn more: [Entitlements](/features/entitlements/introduction)

### 6. **Refund Action Disabled for Fully Refunded Payments**

The **Refund** action on a payment is now disabled once a payment is fully refunded. Previously the button remained clickable and surfaced an error after submission — now the state is communicated upfront, with a tooltip explaining why the action is unavailable.

This is a UI-only change; the underlying refund API behaviour is unchanged.
