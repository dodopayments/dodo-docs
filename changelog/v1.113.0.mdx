## Breaking Changes

### 1. **Przelewy24 (P24) Discontinued**

We have stopped supporting **Przelewy24 (P24)** as a payment method. It is no longer offered at checkout, and `przelewy24` is no longer an accepted value in `allowed_payment_method_types`.

- **What changed:** Przelewy24 will not appear on any checkout, and requests that pass `przelewy24` in `allowed_payment_method_types` will ignore that value.
- **Who is affected:** Merchants that explicitly listed `przelewy24` for Polish checkouts.
- **What to do:** Remove `przelewy24` from any `allowed_payment_method_types` arrays. To keep serving Polish customers, use [BLIK](/features/payment-methods/europe#blik-poland), which also settles in **PLN** (Polish Złoty).

<Note>
BLIK remains fully supported for Poland. Like Przelewy24, it requires a **PLN** billing currency — enable [Adaptive Currency](/features/adaptive-currency) so customers who are quoted in EUR are billed in PLN and BLIK becomes available.
</Note>
