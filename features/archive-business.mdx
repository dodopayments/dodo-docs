## Introduction

Archiving retires a business you no longer trade under. The business disappears from your dashboard, its API keys stop working, and its checkout, payment links, storefront, and Customer Portal stop serving your customers.

Archiving is not deletion. Dodo Payments is your [Merchant of Record](/features/mor-introduction), so your payments, refunds, invoices, and tax records must be retained for compliance long after you stop trading. Archiving closes the business to new activity while those records stay intact.

<Warning>
Archiving is permanent. There is no way to restore an archived business, and support cannot reverse it for you. If you only want to retire one brand and keep trading under the others, archive the brand instead. See [Multi-Brand Setup](/features/multi-brands).
</Warning>

## Eligibility Requirements

A business can only be archived once it is fully settled. Before the option becomes available, Dodo Payments checks seven requirements and shows you a live checklist of each one. You clear the outstanding items yourself, using the same tools you already use day to day.

| Requirement | Cleared when | How to clear it |
|---|---|---|
| **Dormancy** | The business has had no payment activity in the last 180 days | Wait out the window. The checklist shows the date you become eligible. |
| **Payout Wallet** | Every currency balance is exactly zero | Withdraw your remaining funds. See [Account Summary and Payout Wallet](/features/account-summary-payout-wallet). |
| **Payouts** | No payout is still in progress, on hold, or awaiting initiation | Wait for each payout to settle. See [Payout Structure](/features/payouts/payout-structure). |
| **Disputes** | No dispute is open or challenged | Resolve or accept every dispute. See [Disputes](/features/transactions/disputes). |
| **Refunds** | No refund is pending or under review | Wait for each refund to complete. See [Refunds](/features/transactions/refunds). |
| **Subscriptions** | No subscription is pending, active, on hold, or paused | Cancel each subscription. See [Subscriptions](/features/subscription). |
| **Customer Balances** | No customer holds a cash balance | Zero each balance through the [Customer Wallet](/features/customer-wallet) ledger. |

<Note>
A subscription scheduled to cancel at its next billing date still blocks archiving until it actually ends. Dodo Payments never cancels subscriptions on your behalf, so you stay in control of what your customers are charged.
</Note>

Every payment counts towards the dormancy window, whatever its outcome. A failed or abandoned payment attempt restarts the 180 days just as a successful one does.

Customer credit grants do not block archiving. Only cash balances do. Credits expire on their own schedule.

The checklist is evaluated live every time you open it, so refresh it after you clear an item to see the requirement turn green.

## How to Archive Your Business

Only the **Owner** of a business can archive it. Editors and Viewers cannot, even though they can see the checklist. Ownership cannot be transferred, so if you are not the owner, ask them to complete this flow.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the archive checklist">
Go to **Settings → Business** and select **Archive Business**. The checklist shows every requirement and its current status.
</Step>
<Step title="Clear every outstanding requirement">
Work through the items that have not passed, following the resolution links on each one. Return to the checklist and refresh it as you go.
</Step>
<Step title="Request the confirmation code">
Once all seven requirements pass, request a confirmation code. Dodo Payments emails a 6-digit code to the owner's registered email address.

The code expires after 10 minutes. You can request a new one every 60 seconds, and you have 3 attempts to enter it correctly before you need a fresh code.
</Step>
<Step title="Enter the code to confirm">
Enter the code to archive the business. This is the point of no return: the business is archived as soon as the code is accepted.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What Happens After Archiving

**For you**

- The business no longer appears in your dashboard or in the business switcher. If you have other businesses, you are returned to those.
- Live mode and live payments are switched off.
- Every API key for the business stops working, in both live mode and test mode. Cached credentials can take a few minutes to expire, so an integration may briefly keep working after you archive.

**For your customers**

- Checkout sessions, payment links, and static checkout pages stop accepting payments.
- Your storefront stops serving products.
- Customer Portal sessions end, and customers can no longer sign in to manage their purchases.
- License key activation and deactivation stop.

Tell your customers before you archive. Once the business is archived, they have no self-serve route back to their purchase history, and Dodo Payments returns a terminal error rather than a retry.

**For your records**

Payments, refunds, invoices, payouts, and tax filings are retained. Download the invoices and reports you want to keep before you archive, because dashboard access ends the moment the business is archived. Contact [support](/miscellaneous/help-support) if you need a copy of your records afterwards.

## Limits

- **Archiving cannot be undone.** The action is permanent for merchants and support alike.
- **Your last business cannot be archived this way.** If the business is the only one you own, contact [support](/miscellaneous/help-support) to close your account instead.
- **Archiving is all or nothing.** To retire part of your catalogue and keep trading, archive a brand rather than the business.
