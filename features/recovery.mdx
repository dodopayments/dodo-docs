## Overview

Dodo Payments provides two automated recovery features to reduce revenue loss:

| Feature | Trigger | Email Sequence | Recovery Action |
|---------|---------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Abandoned Cart Recovery (ACR)** | Incomplete or failed checkout detected after 60 minutes | Up to 3 emails per sequence | Customer completes payment via recovery link |
| **Subscription Dunning** | Subscription enters `on_hold` (payment failure) or is cancelled by customer | Up to 4 emails per sequence | Customer updates payment method via customer portal |

Both features charge an additional **5% recovery fee** on successfully recovered revenue.

## Abandoned Cart Recovery

Abandoned Cart Recovery detects incomplete or failed checkouts and sends automated email sequences to bring customers back to complete their purchase.

<Frame caption="Abandoned Cart Recovery settings in the dashboard">
<img src="/images/recovery/acr-settings.png" alt="ACR settings page showing enable toggle, discount configuration, and email sequence editor" />
</Frame>

### How ACR Works

<Steps>
<Step title="Detection">
A background process scans for payments that are 60+ minutes old and haven't succeeded. This includes failed payments and checkouts where the customer never attempted payment.
</Step>

<Step title="Classification">
Each abandoned checkout is classified by reason:
- **Payment Failed**: The customer attempted payment but it failed
- **Checkout Incomplete**: The customer visited checkout but never attempted payment
</Step>

<Step title="Email Sequence">
Based on the classification, the system sends up to 3 recovery emails at configured intervals. Each email contains a unique recovery link.
</Step>

<Step title="Recovery">
When the customer clicks the recovery link, they are taken to a recovery page showing their original cart. They can complete payment with a fresh checkout session.
</Step>
</Steps>

### ACR Status Lifecycle

Each abandoned checkout progresses through these states:

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| `abandoned` | Checkout detected as abandoned, no emails sent yet |
| `recovering` | At least one recovery email has been sent |
| `recovered` | Customer completed payment via the recovery link |
| `exhausted` | All emails sent with no recovery, or a newer checkout superseded this one |
| `opted_out` | Customer unsubscribed from recovery emails |

### Configuring ACR

Enable and configure ACR from **Settings** in your dashboard.

<Frame caption="ACR email sequence configuration">
<img src="/images/recovery/acr-email-config.png" alt="Email sequence editor showing subject, body, timing, and enable toggle for each email in the sequence" />
</Frame>

#### Global Settings

| Setting | Description | Default |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| **Enabled** | Master toggle for ACR | Off |
| **Discount Enabled** | Auto-generate a discount code for recovery emails | Off |
| **Discount Percentage** | Percentage discount in basis points (1000 = 10%) | 0 |
| **Discount Valid Days** | How many days the auto-generated discount code remains valid | 5 |

#### Email Sequences

ACR supports two email sequences — **Payment Failed** and **Checkout Incomplete** — each with up to 3 configurable emails.

| Setting | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Enabled** | Toggle individual emails on or off without deleting them |
| **Delay** | How long after abandonment detection to send this email |
| **Subject** | Email subject line with variable placeholders |
| **Body** | HTML email body with variable placeholders |
| **Reply-To** | Email address for customer replies |

**Default email timing:**

| Email # | Default Delay |
|---------|---------------|
| 1 | 1 hour |
| 2 | 24 hours |
| 3 | 72 hours |

#### Email Template Variables

Use these placeholders in your email subject and body:

| Placeholder | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| `{recovery_link}` | URL to the customer portal recovery page |
| `{store_name}` | Your business name |
| `{store_owner_name}` | Your business owner name |
| `{store_contact_email}` | Your support email address |
| `{discount_code}` | Auto-generated discount code (if enabled) |
| `{unsubscribe_link}` | Opt-out link for the customer |

### Recovery Discounts

When discount is enabled, ACR automatically generates a unique, single-use discount code for each abandoned checkout. The discount:

- Is restricted to the product(s) from the original checkout
- Has a configurable expiration period
- Is limited to one redemption
- Is hidden from your dashboard discount list (internal use only)

<Tip>
Start with a modest discount (5-10%) to test conversion impact before increasing. Even a small incentive can significantly improve recovery rates.
</Tip>

### Customer Recovery Experience

When a customer clicks the recovery link in an email, they land on a recovery page that shows:

- The original cart with product details and amount
- A "Pay" button to complete the purchase
- Applied discount code (if enabled)

<Frame caption="Customer recovery page showing cart details and pay button">
<img src="/images/recovery/customer-recovery-page.png" alt="Recovery page in the customer portal showing the abandoned cart details, product name, amount, and a Pay button" />
</Frame>

Clicking "Pay" creates a fresh checkout session with the original cart details. The customer completes payment through the standard checkout flow.

<Info>
Recovery pages are only accessible via the unique link in the recovery email. They are not browsable from the customer portal home page.
</Info>

### ACR Edge Cases

| Scenario | Behavior |
|----------|----------|
| Customer completes purchase before next email | Recovery is detected at send time; checkout is marked as recovered |
| Customer starts a new checkout | Old abandoned checkout is marked as exhausted |
| Customer clicks "Pay" after already recovering | Recovery page shows a confirmation that payment was already completed |
| Customer unsubscribes | Checkout is marked as `opted_out`; no further emails are sent |

## Subscription Dunning

Subscription Dunning automatically sends email sequences to customers whose subscriptions have lapsed, prompting them to update their payment method or reactivate.

<Frame caption="Dunning settings in the dashboard">
<img src="/images/recovery/dunning-settings.png" alt="Dunning settings page showing enable toggle and email sequence configuration for on-hold and cancelled sequences" />
</Frame>

### How Dunning Works

<Steps>
<Step title="Trigger">
Dunning is triggered when a subscription enters one of two states:
- **On Hold**: A renewal payment failed (insufficient funds, expired card, etc.)
- **Cancelled**: The customer cancelled their subscription from the customer portal
</Step>

<Step title="Email Sequence">
Based on the trigger state, the system sends up to 4 dunning emails at configured intervals. Each email includes a link to the customer portal where the customer can update their payment method.
</Step>

<Step title="Recovery">
When the customer updates their payment method through the customer portal, a charge is automatically created for remaining dues. If the payment succeeds, the subscription is reactivated and the dunning attempt is marked as recovered.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Only customer-initiated cancellations from the customer portal trigger the "cancelled" dunning sequence. Merchant-initiated cancellations and system cancellations do not trigger dunning.
</Warning>

### Dunning Status Lifecycle

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| `recovering` | Dunning attempt is active; emails are being sent |
| `recovered` | Customer updated payment method and payment succeeded |
| `exhausted` | All emails sent with no recovery, or subscription state changed unexpectedly |

<Info>
When a dunning attempt is marked as `exhausted`, the subscription state is not modified. The subscription remains in its current state (on hold or cancelled).
</Info>

### Configuring Dunning

Enable and configure Dunning from **Settings** in your dashboard.

<Frame caption="Dunning email sequence editor">
<img src="/images/recovery/dunning-email-config.png" alt="Dunning email configuration showing the on-hold and cancelled sequences with timing and content options" />
</Frame>

#### Email Sequences

Dunning supports two email sequences — **On Hold** and **Cancelled** — each with up to 4 configurable emails.

Each email has the same configurable fields as ACR emails (enabled, delay, subject, body, reply-to).

**Default email timing:**

| Sequence | Email # | Default Delay |
|----------|---------|---------------|
| On Hold | 1 | 10 days |
| On Hold | 2 | 15 days |
| On Hold | 3 | 20 days |
| On Hold | 4 | 25 days |
| Cancelled | 1 | 10 days |
| Cancelled | 2 | 15 days |
| Cancelled | 3 | 20 days |
| Cancelled | 4 | 25 days |

#### Dunning Email Template Variables

| Placeholder | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| `{payment_form_link}` | Link to the customer portal payment method update page |
| `{subscription_id}` | Subscription identifier for reference |
| `{failure_reason}` | Human-readable payment failure reason |
| `{store_name}` | Your business name |
| `{store_owner_name}` | Your business owner name |
| `{store_contact_email}` | Your support email address |

<Info>
Dunning emails do not include an unsubscribe link. They are transactional notifications about an existing billing relationship, following industry standard practice.
</Info>

### Dunning Edge Cases

| Scenario | Behavior |
|----------|----------|
| Customer updates payment method before any email | Recovery is detected but no recovery fee is charged (requires at least 1 email sent) |
| Customer purchases another subscription from same business | Dunning attempt is marked as exhausted |
| Subscription state changes unexpectedly | Worker checks state before each send; marks exhausted or recovered as appropriate |
| All dunning emails exhausted | Dunning attempt is marked as exhausted; subscription state is not modified |

## Recovery Fees

Both ACR and Dunning charge a **5% recovery fee** on successfully recovered revenue. The fee is calculated on the total payment amount including taxes and other fees.

| Fee | Default Rate | Charged When |
|-----|-------------|-------------|
| **ACR Recovery Fee** | 5% | Customer completes payment via recovery link, and at least one recovery email was sent |
| **Dunning Recovery Fee** | 5% | Customer updates payment method and payment succeeds, and at least one dunning email was sent |

<Info>
Recovery fees appear as `abandoned_cart_recovery_fee` and `dunning_fees` entries in your [Balance Ledger](/api-reference/balance-ledger/list-ledger-entries). No fee is charged if the customer recovers independently before any recovery email is sent.
</Info>

## Webhook Events

Recovery events are sent as webhooks to keep your application in sync with recovery lifecycle changes.

### Abandoned Cart Recovery Events

| Event | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `abandoned_checkout.detected` | An abandoned checkout has been detected and recovery has begun |
| `abandoned_checkout.recovered` | A customer completed payment through the recovery flow |

### Dunning Events

| Event | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `dunning.started` | A dunning attempt has been started for a subscription |
| `dunning.recovered` | A subscription has been recovered through dunning |

## Best Practices

- **Start with defaults**: The default email timing and subjects are optimized for common recovery patterns. Customize after you have baseline data.
- **Personalize emails**: Use template variables to make emails feel personal and relevant to the customer.
- **Test in test mode**: ACR and Dunning settings are configured separately for test and live modes. Test your email sequences before going live.
- **Monitor analytics**: Track recovery rates by email to identify which messages drive the most conversions. Disable underperforming emails.
- **Use discounts strategically**: ACR discounts can boost recovery rates, but start small (5-10%) to protect margins.
- **Keep emails concise**: Recovery emails should be short and action-oriented. A clear call-to-action drives better results than lengthy explanations.

<Info>
Recovery is designed to work automatically once enabled. Start with the default settings, monitor your analytics, and iterate based on performance data.
</Info>
