## Home

**Home** is your landing page in the sidebar, with two parts: **Today** and **Your overview**.

### Today

<Frame caption="Today: your day's pulse, hour by hour">
  <img src="/images/analytics/home-today.png" alt="Home Today view with an hourly volume chart, cash position, and next payout tiles" style={{ maxHeight: '500px', width: 'auto' }} />
</Frame>

The day's pulse, hour by hour.

- **Headline chart:** Toggle between **Net volume** and **Gross volume** (defaults to Net volume), plotted hourly against yesterday's same-time line.
- **Cash Position:** Your current wallet balance and next payout date.

<Note>
Both cards show an info tooltip (ⓘ) and an "Updated Xs ago" timestamp, so you always know how fresh the data is.
</Note>

### Your Overview

<Frame caption="Your overview: a fully customizable set of metric cards">
  <img src="/images/analytics/home-overview.png" alt="Home Your overview view with customizable cards for revenue by country, payouts received, top customers by spend, and refunds" style={{ maxHeight: '500px', width: 'auto' }} />
</Frame>

Fully customizable—use **Customize** to choose, reorder, and remove cards.

- **Default cards:** Net volume, Gross volume, Payments, Top customers by spend, New customers, Revenue by country, and Refunds—plus **Average order value** (one-time/usage merchants) or **MRR** and **Net New MRR** (subscription merchants).
- **Controls:**
  - **Period:** Daily/Weekly or a custom range.
  - **Compare:** Previous period or previous month (on by default).
  - **Product:** A single product or a group.

### Filtering & Customization

Each tab has its own filter bar:

- **Time Period:** Preset ranges — **Today**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Month to date**, **Quarter to date**, **Last 6 months**, **All time** — or a custom date range, plus **Daily/Weekly** grouping. New dashboards start on **Last 30 days**.
- **Compare:** Previous period or previous month (on by default). Comparison is unavailable on **All time**, since there is no earlier window to compare against.
- **Product:** Available on the Revenue, Subscriptions, and Retention tabs. Product is multi-select and defaults to **All**.

Your date range and compare selections are remembered per business and synced to your profile, so switching businesses, returning later, or signing in on another device keeps each business on the window you last used.

<Tip>
Use filters to drill down into specific products, time periods, or payment methods for targeted insights.
</Tip>

### Revenue Tab

<Frame caption="Revenue tab: gross/net volume, payouts, and refunds">
  <img src="/images/analytics/revenue-tab.png" alt="Revenue tab showing gross volume, net volume, payouts received, and refunds cards with trend charts" style={{ maxHeight: '500px', width: 'auto' }} />
</Frame>

Track your income, payouts, and dispute activity to understand revenue trends and identify your top-performing markets. Use the **Gross/Net** dropdown to switch the headline figure, chart, and country ranking between gross and net values.

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Gross volume** | Total amount charged across all successful payments in the period, before fees, refunds, or disputes are removed. |
| **Net volume** | Net volume = gross volume − fees − refunds − lost disputes. |
| **Payouts received** | Payout = eligible sales − taxes − fees − refunds − chargebacks. |
| **Refund rate** | Refund rate = refunded amount ÷ gross volume. |
| **Disputes** | Headline is the chargeback rate, gross of outcome: every chargeback received counts, so ones you later win or that get withdrawn don't lower it (this is how card networks judge risk). Won / lost / pending are shown as counts for context, compared across the two periods. Chargeback rate = chargebacks received ÷ payments. |
| **Payments count** | Count of payments with a Successful status. In-progress, failed, and not-initiated attempts are excluded. |
| **Average order value** | AOV = revenue ÷ number of orders, across one-time and usage-based products. |
| **Revenue by product** | Gross revenue attributed to each product, ranked highest to lowest. |
| **Revenue by country** | Revenue attributed to each country, based on the customer's billing country, shown as a full-width card pairing a world map with the ranked country list beside it. Darker countries earned more. The top 150 countries are listed individually, with any remainder grouped into a single "others" row. |

### Customers Tab

<Frame caption="Customers tab: active and new customers, ARPU, and top customers">
  <img src="/images/analytics/customers-tab.png" alt="Customers tab showing active customers, new customers, ARPU, and top customers by spend" style={{ maxHeight: '500px', width: 'auto' }} />
</Frame>

Analyze customer acquisition, retention, and spending patterns to optimize your customer strategy.

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Active customers** | Customers who made a payment or held an active subscription at any point in the period. |
| **New customers** | Customers whose first successful payment with you falls in this period. |
| **ARPU** | ARPU = revenue ÷ active customers. |
| **Top customers** | Customers ranked by revenue in the period, highest first. Each entry is identified by customer name, falling back to email when no name is on record. |
| **New vs. returning** | New = first purchase in this period. Returning = purchased with you in an earlier period. |
| **Modeled LTV** | Estimated revenue from a customer before they churn. |

### Subscriptions Tab

Monitor your recurring revenue and understand how your Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) changes over time.

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **MRR** | Recurring revenue expected in a month, with every plan converted to monthly (a yearly plan counts as 1/12). One-time payments are excluded. |
| **Projected ARR** | Projected ARR = current MRR × 12. |
| **Net New MRR** | Net New MRR = New + Expansion − Contraction − Churned. |
| **Active subscriptions** | Subscriptions actively billing, including those still in a trial. |
| **New MRR** | MRR added by brand-new subscriptions in the period. |
| **Expansion MRR** | Extra MRR from existing customers upgrading or buying add-ons. |
| **Contraction MRR** | MRR lost when existing customers move to a cheaper plan but stay subscribed. |
| **Churned MRR** | MRR lost when customers cancel their subscriptions in the period. |

<Note>
MRR accounts for **concurrent subscriptions** (summed per customer) and prorates by each subscription's paid-through period.
</Note>

### Retention Tab

<Frame caption="Retention tab: churn rates, retention matrix, and NRR/GRR">
  <img src="/images/analytics/retention-tab.png" alt="Retention tab showing the user retention cohort matrix, customer churn rate, revenue churn rate, and net revenue retention" style={{ maxHeight: '500px', width: 'auto' }} />
</Frame>

Monitor customer retention and churn patterns.

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **User retention matrix** | Groups customers by signup month (cohort), then shows the share of each cohort still active in every later month. Retention (month k) = cohort customers active in month k ÷ cohort size. |
| **Customer churn rate** | Share of starting subscribers who cancelled during the period, with the churned-customer count shown beside it. Customer churn rate = churned subscribers ÷ subscribers at start of period. |
| **Revenue churn rate** | Revenue churn rate = churned MRR ÷ starting MRR, where starting MRR is the recurring revenue from existing customers at the start of the period (where the prior period ended). |
| **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)** | Includes upgrades, so it can exceed 100%. NRR = (starting MRR + Expansion − Contraction − Churned) ÷ starting MRR. |
| **Gross Revenue Retention (GRR)** | Excludes upgrades, so it caps at 100%. GRR = (starting MRR − Contraction − Churned) ÷ starting MRR. |
| **Trial conversion** | Trial conversion = trials converted ÷ trials that ended in the period. Only shown if you offer trials. |

### Success Rate Tab

<Frame caption="Success Rate tab: payment and refund success rates with failure analysis">
  <img src="/images/analytics/success-rate-tab.png" alt="Success Rate tab showing payment success rate, payments breakdown, payment failure reasons, and success by payment method" style={{ maxHeight: '500px', width: 'auto' }} />
</Frame>

Monitor payment and refund success rates to identify and resolve transaction issues quickly.

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Payment success rate** | Only processor/network failures count against it; customer-fixable ones (a wrong or expired card) are excluded from the denominator. Payment success rate = succeeded ÷ (attempts − customer-fixable failures). |
| **Payments breakdown** | Splits attempts into total, success, failed (network), failed (customer), and not initiated. |
| **Payment failure reason** | Breakdown of payment failures by reason. |
| **By payment method** | Per method (card, wallet, UPI, BNPL): success rate = succeeded ÷ attempts; volume = succeeded amount. |
| **Checkouts count** | How many checkout sessions customers began, whether or not they completed. |
| **Refund success rate** | The share of initiated refunds that processed successfully. |

<Card title="Transaction Failure Reference" icon="circle-exclamation" href="/api-reference/transaction-failures">
Get all possible transaction failure codes and their reasons.
</Card>

<Warning>
If you notice a drop in success rates or a spike in failures, review the failure reasons and address them promptly to minimize revenue loss.
</Warning>

### Recovery Tab

<Frame caption="Recovery tab: retries, dunning, and abandoned cart recovery">
  <img src="/images/analytics/recovery-tab.png" alt="Recovery tab showing recovery overview, recovery by flow, payment retries, and dunning metrics" style={{ maxHeight: '500px', width: 'auto' }} />
</Frame>

Track how much revenue Dodo Payments recovers on your behalf through automated retries, dunning emails, and abandoned cart recovery.

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Recovery overview** | Recovered revenue over the period, alongside the overall recovery rate. Recovery rate = recovered ÷ recoverable failed amount; hard declines (closed accounts) are excluded from recoverable. |
| **Recovery by flow** | Recovered revenue broken down across retries, dunning, and abandoned cart. |
| **Payment retries** | Retry attempts, successful retries, retry success rate, and subscriptions saved. |
| **Dunning** | Dunning entries, emails sent, recovered revenue, recovery rate, and average time to recover. |
| **Abandoned cart** | Abandoned checkouts, recovered revenue, recovery rate, and average time to recover. |

### Overview Tab

The Overview tab provides a high-level snapshot of the product's financial performance.

- **Total Revenue**: Cumulative revenue generated by this product.
- **Number of Successful Payments**: Total count of successful transactions.
- **Revenue Trend Chart**: A 30-day line chart showing revenue over time. Toggle between cumulative and daily views using the **Show Daily** button.

Below the chart, a **Transactions** table lists every payment for this product:

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Payment ID | Unique identifier for the transaction |
| Status | `Successful` or `Failed` |
| Amount | Transaction amount with currency |
| Date (UTC) | Timestamp of the transaction |
| Payment Method | Card, UPI, or other method used |
| Refund | Option to initiate a refund |

### MRR Tab

A detailed breakdown of your product's Monthly Recurring Revenue.

<Frame caption="MRR stacked bar chart showing monthly revenue composition">
  <img src="/images/product-analytics/mrr.png" alt="MRR tab with stacked bar chart showing new, expansion, and churned MRR by month" style={{ maxHeight: '500px', width: 'auto' }} />

The **MRR chart** visualizes your recurring revenue composition month by month as a stacked bar chart.

Below the chart, metric cards provide detailed insights:

<Frame caption="Detailed MRR breakdown with New, Expansion, Churned, and Net New MRR">
  <img src="/images/product-analytics/mrr-breakdown.png" alt="MRR breakdown showing New MRR, Expansion MRR, Churned MRR, and Net New MRR with trend charts" style={{ maxHeight: '500px', width: 'auto' }} />

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **New MRR** | Revenue from new subscriptions acquired this month, with month-over-month change |
| **Expansion MRR** | Additional revenue from existing subscribers upgrading or adding to their plans |
| **Contraction MRR** | Revenue lost from existing subscribers downgrading without fully cancelling |
| **Churned MRR** | Revenue lost from cancelled subscriptions |
| **Net New MRR** | The net result: New MRR + Expansion MRR − Contraction MRR − Churned MRR |

Each card includes a trend mini-chart and a percentage change indicator compared to the previous month.

## Page Highlight Tiles

Many list pages in your dashboard now display **highlight tiles** at the top, giving you key summary metrics at a glance—without opening the full Analytics dashboard. The tiles respect the page's active filters.

| Page | Highlight Tiles |
|------|-----------------|
| **Products** | All Products, Active Products, Archived Products |
| **Add-ons** | All Add-Ons, Active Add-Ons, Linked to Products |
| **Collections** | All Collections, Active Collections, Archived Collections |
| **Subscriptions** | Active Subscriptions, On Hold, Cancelled |
| **Payments** | Successful Payments, Payment Volume, Failed Payments |
| **Refunds** | Successful Refunds, Refund Volume, Failed Refunds |
| **Disputes** | Disputes Created, Won, Accepted, Lost |

<Note>
"Active" products, add-ons, collections, and meters are those that have participated in at least one successful payment.
</Note>

## Reports

Each report has a **Build Report** button on its respective page. Navigate to the relevant section to access and download the report you need.

Each report can be filtered (by payment method, status, product type, failure type, dispute status, or currency where applicable) and downloaded as a CSV.

| Report Type | Description | Where to find it |
|-------------|-------------|------------------|
| **Payment Report** | Detailed record of all processed payments, including billing address, card details, USD-equivalent amounts, settlement details, fees, and failure codes. | **Transactions → Payments** |
| **Refund Report** | Comprehensive log of all refund activities with detailed refund information. | **Transactions → Refunds** |
| **Disputes Report** | Payment disputes summary with resolution status and evidence details. | **Transactions → Disputes** |
| **Customer Report** | Payment, refund, and dispute summary organized by customer. | **Sales → Customers** |
| **Payout Report** | Summary of payouts made to your account, with a breakdown of fees by ledger event. | **Payouts** |
| **Account Summary Report** | Detailed balance-ledger entries with event type, amount, and references. | **Payouts → Balances** |

  <img src="/images/analytics/create-payment-report.png" alt="Create a Payment Report modal showing time period selection and payment method filters" style={{ maxHeight: '500px', width: 'auto' }} />

## Downloading Reports

<Steps>
<Step title="Navigate to the relevant page">
Go to the page for the report you need using the table above.
</Step>
<Step title="Select a time period">
Click <strong>Build Report</strong> and choose a preset range (Today, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, This month, etc.) or pick a custom date range using the calendar.
</Step>
<Step title="Download">
Click <strong>Download Report</strong>. The report will be exported as a CSV and saved to your device.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Fee data is not available as a standalone report. A breakdown of fees (payment fees, refund fees, and payout fees) is accessible as filter options within the **Account Summary Report** under **Payouts → Balances**.
</Note>

## Best Practices

<Tip>
- Regularly review your analytics to spot trends and anomalies early.
- Use filters to compare performance across products, time periods, or geographies.
- Drill into product-level analytics to understand which products drive the most revenue and which have the highest churn.
- If data appears incorrect or missing, check your filters and date range settings first.
</Tip>

<Warning>
If you notice persistent discrepancies or missing data, contact <a href="mailto:support@dodopayments.com">support@dodopayments.com</a> for assistance.
</Warning>
