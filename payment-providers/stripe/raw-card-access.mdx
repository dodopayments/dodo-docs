<Note>
This guide applies when you connect **Stripe** as your own processor through [Bring Your Own Processor (BYOP)](/features/byop). [Talk to us](mailto:founders@dodopayments.com) if you need help.
</Note>

## Why this is needed

To route payments through your Stripe account while Dodo Payments powers billing on top, Dodo needs to pass card details to Stripe on your behalf using Stripe's **raw card data APIs**.

By default, Stripe accounts can't send raw card numbers (PANs) directly to its APIs. Stripe gates this behind an approval that confirms your account is **PCI DSS compliant**, because handling raw card data brings your systems into PCI scope. You request this access once, per account.

<Info>
You only need to request this for the **Stripe** processor. Once approved, Dodo can route your BYOP traffic through Stripe.
</Info>

## What you'll need

To enable raw card data APIs in **live mode**, Stripe asks for two things:

1. **A short description** of the systems and services in your application that handle card data. If you outsource card handling to a PCI-compliant third party, name that provider.
2. **One** of the following PCI DSS documents:

| Document | When it applies |
|----------|-----------------|
| **SAQ D** | A current, complete PCI DSS Self-Assessment Questionnaire D. The most common option for businesses whose systems touch card data. |
| **Attestation of Compliance (AOC)** | A current PCI DSS Attestation of Compliance for Onsite Assessment, for Level 1 Merchants or Service Providers. |
| **SAQ A** | Only if you fully outsource card data handling to a PCI-compliant third party, accept MOTO payments only, and meet Stripe's other qualifications. |

<Tip>
**Test mode requires no documentation.** You can ask Stripe to enable raw card data APIs for **test mode** right away so you can build and verify your BYOP setup. Live mode still requires the PCI documents above.
</Tip>

## How to request access

<Steps>
<Step title="Prepare your PCI documentation">
Have your current **SAQ D**, **AOC**, or qualifying **SAQ A** ready, along with a brief written description of how your systems handle card data.
</Step>

<Step title="Contact Stripe support">
Open a request with [Stripe Support](https://support.stripe.com/) asking to **enable raw card data APIs** on your account. Attach the documentation and description from the previous step.

For test-mode access, simply ask Stripe to enable raw card data APIs for **test mode only**, with no documentation required.
</Step>

<Step title="Wait for confirmation">
Stripe reviews the request and enables the capability on your account. Approval timelines depend on Stripe's review.
</Step>

<Step title="Connect Stripe in Dodo">
Once Stripe confirms access, return to **Settings → BYOP** in Dodo Payments and add your Stripe connection with your secret key and webhook signing secret.

<Card title="Set up BYOP" icon="shuffle" href="/features/byop">
Follow the full Bring Your Own Processor setup flow.
</Card>
</Step>
</Steps>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Why can't Stripe just enable this by default?">
Sending raw card numbers to an API brings the sending system into PCI DSS scope. Stripe requires proof of PCI compliance before enabling raw card data APIs so that cardholder data stays protected.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I test BYOP before getting live approval?">
Yes. Ask Stripe to enable raw card data APIs in **test mode**, which needs no PCI documentation, and connect Stripe in test mode in Dodo. You'll need the live-mode approval before processing real payments.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How long does approval take?">
It depends on Stripe's review of your documentation. Reach out to Stripe Support early so the approval doesn't block your go-live.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do I need this for Adyen too?">
No. Adyen has its own setup. See the [Adyen guide](/payment-providers/adyen).
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
