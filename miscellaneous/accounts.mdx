## Business Profile

<Steps>
<Step title="Access your Business Profile">
Go to **Settings → Business**.
</Step>
<Step title="Edit and save business details">
Update the business details, such as the business logo and Brand display name (used on invoices), then click **Save Changes**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## User Account

<Steps>
<Step title="Access your User Profile">
Click the icon in the top right, then select **Profile**.
</Step>
<Step title="Edit and save personal details">
Update your personal information (Contact Name, Profile Image, phone number) for a personalized experience, then click **Save Changes**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Change Your Login Email

You can change the email address you use to sign in to your Dodo Payments account. This change applies across all your businesses and requires verification of both your current and new email addresses.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open your profile menu">
Click the icon in the top right corner of the dashboard.
</Step>
<Step title="Go to Profile">
Select **Profile** from the menu.
</Step>
<Step title="Open the Account tab">
Click the **Account** tab.
</Step>
<Step title="Start the email change">
Click **Change email**.
</Step>
<Step title="Verify your current email">
Enter the one-time code sent to your current email address to confirm it's you.
</Step>
<Step title="Enter your new email">
Type the new email address you want to use for sign-in.
</Step>
<Step title="Verify your new email">
Enter the one-time code sent to your new email address to confirm you control it.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
After you change your login email, your old email address can no longer be used to sign in (whether by password, magic link, or emailed code). Any linked social sign-in methods, such as Google or GitHub, will be unlinked and must be reconnected if you want to use them again.
</Warning>

<Note>
Your password, businesses, team access, and verification status remain unchanged. A confirmation email will be sent to your previous email address so you're notified of the change.
</Note>

## Managing Multiple Businesses

Merchants with more than one business can easily add and verify additional businesses through their Dodo Payments account.

**Workflow: Add a New Business**

<Steps>
<Step title="Go to the Home page">
Navigate to the **Home** page.
</Step>
<Step title="Open the Add New menu">
In the top-left corner, next to your current business name, click the **+** icon.
</Step>
<Step title="Select Add New">
An **Add New** pop-up will appear. Click **Add New**.
</Step>
<Step title="Open the Add New Business/Brand panel">
A panel titled **Add New Business/Brand** will open on the right side of the screen. Click **Add New Business**.
</Step>
<Step title="Complete the business creation process">
Follow the on-screen prompts to complete the business creation process.
</Step>
</Steps>
